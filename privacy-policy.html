This Privacy Policy applies to personal information collected through the USStockEdge website, including the landing page, WhatsApp invite links, and visitor-logging tools used on those pages.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect:

  • Information you choose to send in WhatsApp after opening our invite link (such as your phone number, which is then processed by Meta/WhatsApp)
  • Technical visit data collected automatically when pages load: IP address, user agent, page URL, referring URL, and UTM campaign parameters if present in the link you clicked
  • Approximate location and network details derived from your IP address through a third-party lookup (currently IPinfo), such as country, region, city, and ASN
  • A session identifier stored in a first-party cookie so we can connect a later WhatsApp-invite click to the same visit

There is no name/email sign-up form on the current landing page. If you message us or join the WhatsApp group, Meta also processes that activity under WhatsApp’s own terms.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To operate the website and the optional WhatsApp group
  • To deliver educational market-data recaps to the WhatsApp group
  • To understand where traffic comes from (including advertising campaign parameters) and to measure whether a visitor opened the WhatsApp invite
  • To contact you with service-related updates if we have a way to reach you

If you arrived from an advertisement, campaign parameters (such as utm_source) may be stored with your visit so we can see which ads led to a WhatsApp click. We do not sell that data.

3. WhatsApp and Third Parties

Our daily analysis is delivered through WhatsApp, a messaging platform operated by Meta Platforms, Inc. By joining the WhatsApp group, your phone number and any messages you send in the group are subject to WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

We encourage you to review WhatsApp's privacy policy at whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy.

We use service providers that necessarily receive some data to operate the site: our hosting provider, our database host, IPinfo (IP lookup), and Meta/WhatsApp (if you open the invite). We do not sell your personal information to data brokers or affiliate networks.

4. Data Retention

We retain WhatsApp-related contact information for as long as you remain a member of the USStockEdge WhatsApp group or until you request deletion. Visitor logs (IP address, user agent, campaign parameters, and WhatsApp-click timestamps) are retained only as long as needed to operate and secure the site, and are removed on request where we can identify your records.

If you leave the WhatsApp group or request that your data be deleted, we will remove your name, email address, and phone number from our records within 30 days.

5. No Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to any third party for commercial purposes. Your data is used exclusively to operate and deliver the USStockEdge service.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect the personal information you provide. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data.

You are responsible for keeping your WhatsApp account secure. If you believe your information has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights with respect to your personal information:

  • Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You may request that we correct any inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: You may request that we delete your personal data at any time.
  • Withdrawal of consent: You may leave the WhatsApp group at any time with no obligation or penalty.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 10.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has submitted their information through our form, please contact us and we will remove it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our service after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
